Title:
DualNet-AL: A Bi-Network Meta-Learning Framework for Multi-Class Active Learning

Authors (arXiv format):
Himansh Raj, Om Narayan Pandey, Jaswinder Singh

Abstract (1340 characters; arXiv limit 1920):
Pool-based active learning reduces annotation cost by letting a model choose which unlabelled images to label, but single-criterion strategies are brittle: uncertainty sampling picks redundant or outlying samples, a single model reinforces its own errors, and at small budgets the model's uncertainty is itself unreliable. We present DualNet-AL, which trains two architecturally different convolutional networks, scores every unlabelled sample with five complementary signals (uncertainty, inter-network disagreement, representativeness, diversity and class balance), fuses them with weights adapted online by a meta-learner, and, in its second version (v2), combines a budget-aware prior that favours typical samples while labels are scarce with cluster-based batch construction. The configuration was chosen on validation data only and evaluated on held-out seeds and the official test splits. Across Fashion-MNIST, SVHN and CIFAR-10 (100->900 labels), DualNet-AL v2 improves the area under the learning curve over random sampling by 2.41, 4.38 and 1.46 percentage points, winning on each of the 3 held-out seeds of every dataset; it is the best of nine methods on SVHN and CIFAR-10 and more than doubles the worst-class recall of random sampling on SVHN (40.5% vs 16.7%). Code, per-run logs and a one-command demo are publicly available.
